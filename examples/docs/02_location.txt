Mara lives in Seattle, Washington, in a third-floor walk-up in the Ballard neighborhood. The apartment is small but bright, with a balcony that catches afternoon light and a view of maple trees lining a quiet side street. She grew up near the Willamette River in Portland, and she still visits when she can, usually taking the train south on Friday evenings. On weekends she walks to the farmers market, shops for flowers she forgets to water, and claims the neighborhood bakery knows her order before she reaches the counter.
