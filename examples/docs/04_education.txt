Mara studied industrial design at Rhode Island School of Design and graduated in 2011 with a thesis on modular kitchen tools for small apartments. She chose RISD after a summer internship at a furniture studio convinced her she wanted to shape everyday objects, not only screens. During school she worked part-time at a campus makerspace, teaching first-years how to use laser cutters and sewing machines without cutting corners on safety. Years later she still sketches products on paper before opening design software, a habit she says keeps her ideas from getting too neat too soon.
