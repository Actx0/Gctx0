Mara Ellison was born on November 2, 1988, in Portland, Oregon, and spent most of her childhood bouncing between school, the public library, and long rainy walks with her parents. Friends and colleagues usually call her Mara, though her grandmother still insists on Marabelle, a middle name she rarely uses. She carries a United States passport, prefers her coffee without sugar, and keeps a small notebook of half-finished sketches in every bag she owns.
