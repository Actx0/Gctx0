Mara bikes to work most weekdays and plays weekend pickup soccer whenever the weather holds. At home she keeps a small ceramic studio in a converted closet, where she throws uneven mugs and gifts the ones that survive the kiln. She listens to long-form podcasts on her commute, usually interviews with makers and city planners, and she reads about a dozen novels a year, leaning toward quiet contemporary fiction. In the evenings she sometimes sketches Theo's toys from memory, claiming it is practice, though Jonah says it is mostly an excuse to stay up past bedtime.
