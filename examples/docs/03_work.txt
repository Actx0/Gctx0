Mara works as a product designer at a climate-tech startup that builds tools for tracking building energy use. She leads a team of three designers and spends most of her week between Figma files, customer interviews, and long reviews with engineers. Her focus areas are data visualization and mobile prototyping, and she shipped the company's first customer dashboard after eighteen months of iteration. She prefers deep work in the mornings, keeps Mondays for planning, and usually leaves the office early on Fridays to ride her bike while there is still daylight.
